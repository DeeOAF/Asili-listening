# Work Planning prototype (Asili field cascade)

Single-file HTML prototype. `node build.js` stitches src/template.html + style.css + engine.js + app.js into work-planning.html (served by Vercel at /work-planning/) and work-planning.artifact.html (the shareable claude.ai page: no doctype/head, logo embedded). The shared page is https://claude.ai/artifact/C7E1PDLzaTQNXqHvxHhCfE; after a change, rebuild and republish work-planning.artifact.html to that URL so the link stays the same. `node test.js` runs the engine tests. No dependencies.

## Rules that must hold
- All data is generated (engine.js generateData, hashRng for deterministic history). Never present it as real. Keep the "made-up data" label.
- Hierarchy is always Region > Area > Zone > Territory. Each TA holds one territory; screens name the territory first and the TA second.
- DRMs are treated as RMs. YFOs are excluded. Duka Assistants (DAs, 2 per zone) are a separate branch under the ZS, beside the territories.
- Order of work in the engine: (1) per TA and priority: requirement, delivered, pace line, distance (calcTA); (2) share a zone gap among TAs who are behind for season targets (allocateZone); (3) areas and zones are sums of TA numbers (aggregate); (4) only then turn each TA's ask into hours (planTA): weights over unmet priorities, spare hours to ageing check-ins.
- Score = per TA min(1, delivered / pace-line expectation), weighted by the RM's weights over the priorities that unit is accountable for. Bands: on track >= 95, slipping >= 82, behind >= 60, critical < 60.
- Two workforces, two priority sets. TAs (credit sales and extension) work to the RM's TA set; Duka Assistants (cash sales at the dukas) work to the RM's separate duka set (S.dprios, WP.defaultDukaPriorities, passed as computeRegion opts.duka). Each set's weights total 100 on their own. Duka measures: dsales (cash sales, KES), tms (TMS validations), invvar (inventory variances resolved), expiry (seed expiry risks cleared), duka (compliance visits).
- Units above a territory show two scores, TAs and Duka, never one blended number. A territory has only the TA score; a DA only the duka score.
- DA plans use the same accept / edit / send-to-ZS approval flow as TA plans. ACs and ZSs accept each set (TA and duka) as the RM set it, or adjust its weights for their area or zone (+/-20 points, total 100, reason, approved one level up). Duka weight changes reach DA plans via computeRegion opts.dukaZoneWeights; scores stay on the RM's weights. In app.js, ACC_SET ('ta' or 'duka') picks the set for the acceptance code; buttons carry data-set.
- "Met target" for areas, zones and territories = 95% of the pace line on every priority the unit is accountable for.
- AC (area) and ZS (zone) may change weights only: +/-20 points, total 100, reason required, approved one level up. Scores stay on the RM's weights.
- Roles: Viewing as RM / AC / ZS / TA / DA scopes every view; priorities are read-only below the RM.
- Pillars: every priority names one of eight pillars in p.pillar (trees = Trees & Poultry, enrolment, repayment, extension, hvc, aggregation, duka, compliance). A measure's home pillar (PILLARS[k].metrics) is only the default; use WP.pillarOfP(p), never pillarOf(p.metric), wherever a priority's pillar is shown. Publishing a week is blocked if any priority lacks one. trees (tree and poultry orders) and aggr (produce aggregated) are illustrative TA measures generated from their own hash streams so other numbers do not move.
- Saving: on claude.ai the page declares the db and user capabilities and keeps shared state in two documents, wp/plan (priorities, duka sets, weeks, publishing, weight acceptance) and wp/plans (TA and DA plans), with maps stored as [{k, v}] lists. Every click, input and change schedules one debounced save (scheduleSave); other viewers' saves arrive live through onSnapshot. View-only viewers never write. Opened as a local file nothing is saved. "How it works" has a confirmed "Clear saved changes".
- Farm Planner: the Enrolment pillar links out to the Tupande Farm Planner (FARM_URL https://asili-listen.vercel.app/planner/, relative /planner/ when served from that site): on enrolment priority rows, enrolment plan blocks, a card on a territory's week and in the More menu. Links open in a new tab.
- Colours in the headers: Kakamega Green for the poster band and section badges, Zaituni for the context band and view icons (dark ink text on Zaituni, never white).
- Weeks: WEEKS is a calendar (0 closed, 1 last week with the live results, 2 this week, 3+ upcoming). Planning weeks (>= 2) start as Not started; the RM copies the previous week's TA and duka sets (S.wkPrios / S.wkDprios) or starts from defaults, edits, then publishes. Below the RM a planning week shows only once published. Never edit prios() / dpriosWk() for a week that has not been started: before that they return the previous week's sets.
- UX conventions: the More menu never renders open and closes on pick, outside click or Escape; actions confirm with toast(); drilling below your own level shows a Back link; the role switcher is labelled as a demo control.
- Keep it simple: each role has one home (RM/AC/ZS: their unit with priorities and the units below it, lowest first; TA/DA: My week). Main nav is Home, Priorities, Plans to approve; everything else sits under More. Answer first in plain words, reasons inside "How is this worked out?".

## Files
- src/engine.js: window.WP (METRICS, PILLARS, generateData, calcTA, computeRegion, aggregate, planTA, focusOf, reviewUnit, history, histUnit, weekCheck).
- src/app.js: state S, scope helpers (sreg, sres, scopeIds), simple views (viewHome, unitCard, prioList, childList, territoryHome, daHome, viewPrioMerged), detailed views under More, approval workflows (browser state only).
- src/style.css: tokens (single light look on a white page, no dark theme by choice), components. The last layer, "White refresh", is the current look: one Kakamega Green poster band per screen (unit name, round score badges, the one yellow arc), notebook sheets for priorities and lists (red margin rule, hairline rows, round line icons per pillar or duka measure). Follow the tupande-design skill.
